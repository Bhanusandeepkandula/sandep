# Track

Personal expense tracker (PWA). Log spending, set budgets, split bills, import statements, and review monthly reports.

## Register

product

## Users

People who track day-to-day spending on their phone, often as an installed home-screen app. They open it briefly to log or check totals, not to browse.

## Core jobs

- Log an expense in under 10 seconds
- See what this month looks like vs budget
- Find and edit past transactions
- Review spending reports and history

## Tone

Calm, precise, trustworthy. Numbers first. No marketing language in UI.
